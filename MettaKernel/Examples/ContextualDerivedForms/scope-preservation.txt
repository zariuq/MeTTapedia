Logical-variable allocation through source execution
===================================================

UnifyingTransitionPreservation.transition_wellScoped proves allocation support
for every independent transition of the maintained completed-operand machine.
Running terms, branch substitutions, saved frames, retained cells and output
stay below the reached variable-allocation supply. Public rows, private clauses
and unread input are templates; their existing copy/use boundaries allocate
their logical identities.

UnifyingPrefixScopes lifts the result to actual bounded endpoints, arbitrary
source paths, suspended configurations and the next scoped request. No producer
termination or successful answer is required. Future activation names cannot
alias returned values or the keys of their branch substitutions.

The ordinary primitive-reply law is proved in StdLib beside the dispatcher.
This matters for ordinary builds: Lean generates a large match splitter using
the defining environment for local declarations but the post-import environment
for imported declarations. A later theorem's local allowance does not change
the imported realization's options. Keeping the law with the definition avoids
special client build commands. The existing primitive definitions and earlier
proofs remain byte-identical; clients instantiate the checked law.

Source admission and support preservation are distinct obligations. To allocate
scope n freshly against retained cells, their variables must already be below
n. Merely being below the post-entry counter n+1 would allow reuse of n itself.
next_request_apart_from_cells derives the required disjointness from the
previous configuration's invariant and the actual retained supply.

PeTTaScopePreservationControls includes every-fuel consumers for the lambda
collision and arbitrary-input fixtures. Negative controls retain raw unscoped
entry, a stale cell supply, missing primitive cell support and same-scope reuse.
These conditions describe identity allocation; they introduce no runtime check
and no restriction on legal source spellings.

ScopeModel imports these checked controls and retains its seven executable
observations. This support result is not source-compiler adequacy or a C
correspondence theorem. Open-answer observation and inverse transport are now
checked separately in open-answer-readout.txt. Recursive lambda translation,
static computed-head admission and remaining source profiles stay separate.
The logical identity supply is separate from evaluator fuel.
