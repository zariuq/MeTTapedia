Complete streaming binding refinement and allocated-variable support

The actual streaming controller now preserves two whole-frontier invariants.
They include the active task, every pending alternative, saved source frames,
collection markers and answer banks, and completed root answers. The variable
support invariant additionally includes retained cells and printed output.
Marker association is separate from both predicates.

StreamingBindingInvariant and StreamingBindingPreservation reuse the existing
actual Unifying transition-refinement theorem. Source phases, delivery,
placement, alternative expansion, collection closure, first demand and caught
faults preserve idempotent branch substitutions refining the original record.
The proof does not restrict itself to the currently selected answer.

StreamingBindingReachability derives the property for actual entry, arbitrary
finite paths and every bounded endpoint. Every returned answer and pending
evaluation therefore earns idempotence. The source/runtime readout theorem can
consume a reached answer without taking idempotence as an extra caller-supplied
fact. enterRefined and advanceRefined carry the propositions with the actual
state; execution performs no validation scan.

StreamingScopeInvariant and StreamingScopePreservation transfer the existing
source allocation-support mathematics to all streaming transitions. Untouched
payloads weaken under the actual monotone allocation supply. Collected answer
copies earn support below the actual increased counter. Retained cells and
output come from the source phase's proved reached world. Public rows, private
clauses and unread input remain templates copied at their existing boundaries;
no global template scan or reserved source spelling is introduced.

StreamingScopeReachability proves actual entry, loaded-program prefixes,
subsequent entry hygiene and freshness against retained cells and returned
binding/value records. A genuinely unused activation is required for the
apartness result. A mere post-entry upper bound would permit an old cell to
share the newly chosen identity. These hygiene facts do not authorize dropping
a paid residual agenda or replacing an unfinished request with a new one.

The generic first-selected suffix/update invariant laws are owned by
Data/List/FirstSelectionInvariant. Both binding and support proofs use those
same list operations. Missing markers still interrupt rather than yielding a
fabricated successful suffix or update.

Negative controls cover invalid bindings hidden in alternatives, a source
frame, a collection marker and completed answers. Support controls include a
future identity hidden in a bank or caller, and a cell that satisfies the
post-entry counter but aliases a reused activation. These are proofs about
construction invariants, not extra runtime rejection rules for programmers.

OutputPlanModel now retains proof-carrying world support between directives
and binding refinement through its actual bounded execution. It consumes the
same compiler, state and controller as before; its original 49 observations
remain unchanged. The later collection fixture adds eight more. The propositions
are erased rather than implemented by another
matcher, solver or runtime validity pass.

Region-marker uniqueness/association is now checked separately in
streaming-regions.txt. Cross-branch source collection correspondence remains
open, as do whole compiler/control simulation,
list-view/typed/lambda/dynamic regions, recursive HE demand, richer transform
authority and the original authored-surface obligations. These Lean invariants
do not supply C/machine-code correspondence or native performance evidence.
