Open answers: sharing observations and inverse transport

FirstOccurrence proves supported relabeling laws for arbitrary finite lists.
MORK/AtomRenaming and MORK/VariantReadout adapt those laws to the existing
OSLFCore Atom used by MORK and the maintained unifying PeTTa source model.
They do not change HE's separate source carrier or introduce another Atom.

VariantReadout.normalize_equal_iff_variant preserves and reflects independently
specified bidirectional variable renaming. The canonical labels are first
occurrence positions; repeated variables share their first position. Constants,
expression shape, order and multiplicity remain exact. Legal decimal variable
names require no reserved namespace.

joint observes a whole answer bundle with one variable basis. each observes
every answer separately. Joint equality implies individual equality, but the
converse loses sharing between answers. These are distinct observer policies;
neither is selected as Prime's final public display policy.

PeTTa/AnswerReadout connects the observation to actual source outcomes:
- normalize_rename_apart hides an allocated scope without merging variables.
- each_copies uses the actual collection copier and resolved branch values.
- restore_read recovers completed outcomes, faults and suspended configurations.
- packAnswers retains each branch's substitution, including constraints absent
  from its visible value; restore_packAnswers recovers the original answers.
- run_after_transport preserves any source continuation and every finite budget
  after inverse transport. It does not erase the work of building the readout.

Displayed labels alone are not executable references into a retained world.
Inverse transport preserves an alias to a stored cell; replaying display labels
loses it. Likewise two identical displayed values can retain different branch
constraints and yield different outer answers. Erasing those constraints is
observable. Packet construction and restoration are lossless transport, not
an untrusted-wire authentication or packet-validation theorem.

ReadoutModel.lean consumes eight positive/negative observations. The independent
open-answer-aliases.metta fixture has six observations: internal aliasing,
distinct variables, isolation between collected answers, sharing within one
collected answer, repeated cell reads and legal decimal source names. Its
expected answers are true, false, false, true, true, false.

These results do not prove the whole source compiler, evaluator equivariance,
textual print/read recovery or C correspondence. Recursive lambda translation,
eta expansion, static computed-head staging and native partial arities remain
separate source-admission obligations.
