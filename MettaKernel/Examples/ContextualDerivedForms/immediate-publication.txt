Immediate publication: checked semantic scope
=============================================

ImmediateStorePublication defines append and ordered filtering independently
of the existing prepared protocol. staged_fuses proves their public replies
and reached worlds agree, deriving readiness from the operation's own checked
admission. adaptive_fuses and adaptive_receipt_erasure lift this connection to
all finite adaptive clients of that interface. The serial issuer state is
private; unrecorded_serial_unchanged proves no ticket is issued in ordinary mode.

Recording capacity and effect capacity are distinct. An unavailable requested
record can make an observable failure, so the erasure theorem requires recording
capacity when recording is enabled. A caller that actually observes the receipt
is outside the erasing observer. Both distinctions have checked counterexamples.

ImmediateStorePrefixes uses the existing cursor provider/client machinery.
Its prefix and resume theorems retain pending control, reached state, completion
status and reply history. The two-operation executable preserves its first
append when its second append is refused, including across a pause. Equal
declared request prices preserve the account; this does not measure or remove
the implementation's internal administrative work.

Step/ImmediatePublication realizes a successful immediate operation as an
admitted existing tcalc store effect. No target execution or desired equivalence
is supplied as a premise. Consistency of ambient knowledge remains an explicit
calculus hypothesis. Source error/control events need their own interpretation.

ContextualTransform/ImmediateRemoval connects first-occurrence deletion and
completed scoped PeTTa selection to immediate contraction. The selected
identities are not another shared unifier: every PeTTa row retains its own
renaming and match result. Continued presence does not guarantee first-match
order after an intervening insertion; the negative control shows that difference.

Run the derived model from the Lean project root:

  lake env lean --run ../../MettaKernel/Examples/ContextualDerivedForms/ImmediatePublicationModel.lean

Compare stdout with ImmediatePublicationModel.expected. This consumer executes
the Lean definitions; it is neither a separate MeTTa evaluator nor a proof that
CeTTa's C follows them. C instruction counts and timing remain independent
qualification obligations. Costs, full invocation contexts, lifetime refinement,
and both authored surface presentations remain open in the larger goal.
