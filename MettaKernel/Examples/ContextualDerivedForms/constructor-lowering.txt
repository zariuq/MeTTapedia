Retained constructor decisions and ordered operands

ConstructorDataHeads emits an unknown literal head as ordinary quoted nullary
data or native-cons construction of its head and operands. Source control
forms and known calls remain operations. The expansion uses the existing
cons-atom primitive and introduces no fresh variables or new native operation.

ConstructorEvaluation independently describes completed operand schedules
using actual source paths. Each alternative carries its own substitution;
suffixes run in discovery order through the reached shared world. ConstructorDirect
and ConstructorRealization reach identical return packets and world above
arbitrary saved callers. Normality follows from the actual unifier invariant.
ConstructorContinuations proves agreement under every subsequent allowance
after the two finite administrative prefixes. Equal initial fuel need not
have equal cutoffs; native instruction counts and timing are not measured.

ConstructorSpine lifts arbitrary operand prefixes without requiring termination.
ConstructorFaults transports escaping faults to the same caller recovery and
reached effects. UnifyingRecoveryFrames retains inner runtime handlers, and
argument waiters discard unfinished operand packets. General reflection of
arbitrary unfinished constructor runs remains open.

frozen_step and run_frozen prove the exact return boundary under any future
registry, including the caller frames, world, bindings, I/O and identity supply.
run_raw_nullary proves eight steps to that boundary through the ordinary
unknown-head route. Quotation takes one. These are logical source charges;
finite cutoffs differ, and neither theorem measures native instructions or time.

UnifyingCompilerLocals proves properties of the actual binder and unifier:
- FreshLocal excludes a key from both existing keys and existing bound values.
- fresh_local_bindVar retains the original substitution literally as its tail.
- fresh_local_resolve preserves resolution of source terms avoiding the local.
- unifyBinding_fresh_local derives the successful binding with the actual
  unifier's automatically supplied allowance, rather than assuming success.
- erase_new_local recovers the original substitution, and idempotence is kept.

UnifyingLocalProjection proves lookup/resolution projection and commutation
with ordinary bindings at other keys. CleanValues excludes latent references
to the local from stored bound values. These are sufficient algebraic laws;
whole evaluator/context erasure and general unifier projection remain open.
They do not justify dropping arbitrary source branch constraints.

PeTTaConstructorControls exercises later registration, an ordinary known call,
the changed cutoff, empty-result control and input consumption. It also checks
the actual fresh binder and the clash caused by reusing an existing source key.
ConstructorModel executes nine observations. StagingModel executes eight,
including both corrected future-head cases. ConstructorOperandsModel reads
constructor-operands.metta through the shared reader and executes eight
independently specified observations. Binder and case patterns stay syntax;
their initializers and executable bodies are prepared in their source roles.

constructor-faults.metta distinguishes root-answer retention, an answerless
operand and an error-only operand; earlier writes survive all three. Its first
observation exposed a Lean recovery bug present with and without expansion:
an outer singleton clause collector overwrote an inner completed root prefix.
The corrected recovery combines root prefixes in discovery order and clears
tentative values at operand/continuation boundaries. PeTTaConstructorFaultControls
checks direct and expanded roots, nested collectors and tentative-value rejection.
General recursive operand streaming is still outside this completed-operand
profile. No public Prime syntax or C correspondence is selected here.

constructor-streaming-frontier.metta records that remaining boundary with
observable writes. SWI and the retained native snapshot produce the same
answers and event order left,suffix,right,suffix. The completed-operand profile
produces left,right,suffix,suffix: it finishes choose before running the suffix.
The independent expectation is unchanged. This calibration remains unqualified
for that profile and requires a streaming operand/continuation connection;
the completed-schedule constructor theorem is not full SWI source adequacy.

PeTTa/Streaming now supplies that schedule in a distinct profile, and
StreamingModel reproduces the required source order. The older profile's
observation remains recorded separately. Whole compiler adequacy still needs
compiled output constraints, as source-initializer-constraint.metta demonstrates;
streaming alone does not make post-return binding match SWI's early constraint.
