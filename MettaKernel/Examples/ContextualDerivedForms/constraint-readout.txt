Canonical source readout and exact streaming caller re-entry

The source equation solver and the maintained Lean streaming unifier can
choose different most-general representatives. Equality of raw variable names
is therefore too strong; comparing each value independently is too weak when
values share variables.

Logic/LP/MutualInstantiation proves the generic fact using the existing
substitution types: mutual instances of finite terms differ only by variable
renaming. The maps come from the actual substitutions. A retraction forces
variable images on the observed support and proves their injectivity there;
no global permutation or restriction on unused coordinates is assumed.
For mutually general substitutions, one pair of maps serves every term.

PeTTa/UnifyingConstraintReadout earns the runtime environment's most-general
property from its actual first-lookup presentation. The independent source
solver contributes its existing MGU theorem. Uniform variable maps connect
their actual readouts, and the existing VariantReadout joint observer agrees
on every finite observation bundle. This includes values, saved caller terms
and other atoms observed under that branch's record, preserving their sharing.
The result specializes to the source record and actual solved substitution
after a successful literal result gate.

This is one branch's binding/readout connection. Independent branch copies,
scope allocation and entire effectful language execution require their own
correspondence. The result selects neither a public variable-printing policy
nor Prime syntax.

StreamingAnswerTransport reuses AnswerReadout.BranchPacket. Each branch keeps
its actual substitution and inverse basis. Re-entry restores the original
values before the actual streaming caller runs. The entire re-entered state
is recovered, including world, counters, private code, older alternatives and
scope markers. Every subsequent runner budget and outcome therefore agrees.
This optional interface is not a mandatory per-answer evaluator pass, and
construction/transport cost is not asserted to vanish.

Controls use the actual source solver on x=y and runtime unification of the
equivalent y=x. Raw readouts differ, but their general joint observation law
holds. A caller can distinguish executing display labels without their inverse
basis, and can distinguish dropping a binding absent from the displayed value.
These are changes to actual Streaming.run outcomes, not only to printed text.

ConstraintReadoutModel executes six observations. It distinguishes the raw
representatives, checks joint sharing, restores the caller's value, exercises
both corruptions and runs an effectful caller. One earlier marker remains and
one routed addition is performed. The mathematical all-budget result covers
arbitrary saved frames and older jobs without assuming their termination.
