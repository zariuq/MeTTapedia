ORDINARY-ROW PROOF BANK: REUSE AND RETIREMENT BOUNDARIES

The accepted work survives a change of native backend. It specifies what row
firing means, how packed descriptors represent that meaning, and which output
and memory effects a caller may rely on. The unfinished GCC-generated adapter
machine proof is a separate, paused artifact.

Reusable mathematics
--------------------
ordinary_flat.ml defines row/constant lookup independently of flat_fire machine
code. An ordinary make descriptor packs row and column into two 8-bit fields;
a guard packs two such selections into four 8-bit fields. The projection and
ORDINARY_FIRE_REFINEMENT theorems relate this packing to independent guard and
selection semantics under the declared 4-word-row, 24-premise and 32-item bounds.

flat_spec.ml specifies the packed leaf. Its row-base plus column computation
uses 32-bit modular addition. Natural-number addressing is justified only by
the corresponding nonwrapping premises; wrapped-offset controls remain valid
and must survive a new backend.

ordinary_pack_spec.ml separates source extents, destination capacities and
complete packed workspaces. Its result lists include the previous suffixes.
cesta_adapter_semantics.ml proves that those mathematical outputs retain the
independent ordinary-row firing meaning. It needs no linked adapter artifact.

flat_workspace.ml and flat_trace_workspace.ml retain useful exact contracts:
prefix/suffix splicing, word-array splitting, access-region widening and event
composition at a fresh callee-entry baseline. Some are statements about the
HOL x86 memory/event model; they require a correspondence or a new proof before
being used as Lean/Sail theorems.

Independent machine evidence
----------------------------
The canonical traced flat_fire proof and its functional weakening concern the
exact 211-byte leaf. Full-workspace contracts include suffix preservation and
their stated separation/access conditions. The exact Micromath leaf contracts
are also retained. These provide independent comparison implementations and
artifact-specific checks, even after t-ISA replaces the surrounding adapter.

The bank does not prove ORDINARY_PACK_MC_CORRECT or ORDINARY_ADAPTER_MC_CORRECT.
Running its gate cannot certify the paused complete-adapter chain. The old
proofs/check.sh retains that chain's completion requirements and remains separate.

Concrete reuse in the native-refinement lane
--------------------------------------------
1. Give ordinary_flat_fire the independent ordinary-row result as its source
   contract; keep the old leaf as an oracle rather than defining the new result
   by the old implementation.
2. Preserve the word-width and no-wrap distinctions at descriptor/address
   lowering. Copying a natural-number formula is insufficient.
3. Carry full workspace equations through the new consumer: produced prefix,
   untouched suffix, refusal effects and capacity conditions.
4. Preserve the entire executable image required by a caller, including future
   blocks and return targets. The Lean X86CodePlacement.Placed.preserved theorem
   now expresses this view-wide preservation rule; a leaf-local exclusion does
   not discharge it.
5. Add the new t-ISA observations explicitly: fuel, status, ABI, scratch registers
   and no-verdict. They are not covered by the old span ABI contracts.
6. Keep access-trace safety distinct from final-state framing. A bound on final
   writes alone does not establish bounded reads or exclude transient accesses.

Retention and cleanup
---------------------
Keep all bank sources, exact machine images/objects, runtime controls, accepted
receipts, historical source, expanded failed proof attempts and their logs.
The earlier untraced proof remains byte-for-byte history. Kimi's pack/adapter
attempt stays paused; this document starts no further tactic replay.

cleanup-candidates.tsv lists only legacy build-proof OCaml replay products:
compiled interfaces/objects and native proof executables. It excludes machine
objects under proof, source, expanded ML, logs and the accepted bank cohorts.
These products are rebuildable; their hashes identify the cleanup cohort.
The approved removal of all 135 products is recorded in cleanup-result.txt.
Preserved source and evidence hashes matched before and after deletion.
