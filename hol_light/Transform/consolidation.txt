Transform calculus consolidation: evidence inventory
===================================================

This inventory records the mathematical evidence for the consolidation
requirements. The final fresh dependency rebuild and artifact qualification
are separate gates; this inventory alone is not a completion certificate.

Scope
-----

The general core separates preparation, admission, execution and observation.
The structural occurrence and quoted contextual profiles have actual source
interpretations. Represented native states and candidates have prepared-result
closure; exact codecs apply only to those represented carriers. Reflection of
all permitted paths requires candidate coverage at represented states.
Faithful candidate interpretation additionally gives unique path lifting.
An unbounded counted-store source consumer instantiates these laws.
The GSOS connection retains its finite-branching set-observation assumptions.
Native implementation refinement and full rho/PeTTa factorization are separate
consumer and subsequent-work obligations.
