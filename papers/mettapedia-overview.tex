\textbf{Purpose.} MeTTapedia develops reusable formal mathematics, logic, and
AI theory across several proof systems. Its central engineering aim is to
connect executable reasoning with precise semantics and checked evidence:
what a program computes, what a statement means, and why a certificate
licenses that statement should agree. The repository is an active research
library; the presence of a development does not imply that every claimed
result in that development has been proved.

\section*{The project at a glance}

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}>{\raggedright\arraybackslash}p{.22\linewidth}X@{}}
\toprule
Area & Contents and connections \\
\midrule
Mathematical foundations & Probability, information theory, order and
category theory, computability, Knuth--Skilling foundations of inference,
exchangeability, and universal AI. These supply assumptions and structures
used by the reasoning developments. \\
Evidence and reasoning & Probabilistic Logic Networks (PLN), world-model
semantics, Bayesian networks, higher-order logic, and bridges between
derivations, evidence, and probabilistic interpretations. \\
Languages and processes & MeTTa dialects, MeTTaIL, operational semantics,
OSLF, process calculi including the rho calculus, and language interfaces
for Metamath, GF, SUMO, and related systems. \\
Compositional interfaces & Graph-structured lambda theories
(GSLTs), typed translation, contextual substitution, certificate checking,
and cost-sensitive transport. Graph and matrix representations connect
language interfaces with neural and algorithmic work. \\
Applications and case studies & Neural-network and cognitive-architecture
models, formal ethics, four-color and Ramsey developments, and executable
checker kernels. Their verification status varies by result. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

\begin{center}
\small
Mathematical foundations
$\longrightarrow$ language and world-model semantics\\[3pt]
$\longrightarrow$ typed interfaces and checked certificates
$\longrightarrow$ applications
\end{center}
This is a conceptual map, not an import graph. A Bayesian-network inference
rule requires its stated model and independence hypotheses; returning a
number does not justify applying it without them. Likewise, certificate
acceptance must refer to the intended language, claim, and revision.

The flagship library is \src{lean/mettapedia/Mettapedia/}. Separate Lean
projects cover algorithms, support libraries, ethics, four-color and Ramsey
work, with editable external dependencies under \src{lean/externals/} and
standalone projects under \src{lean/standalone/}. Other formal systems have
their own trees: \src{cakeml/}, \src{megalodon/}, \src{mizar/}, and
\src{isabelle/}. The current Lean migration does not validate those other
toolchains.

\section*{Research obligations and integration}

Compilation, regression checks, and admission-free proofs are distinct gates.
Unresolved mathematics remains in the graph WeakProduct construction,
FourColor, and Lean4Lean; Foundation retains its unproved point-generated-frame
height axiom. The migration does not complete these developments.

Lean 4.31 comparisons reproduce the evaluator, canonical-bridge, exporter,
and OSLF regression-fixture failures. The OSLF fixture retains exactly its two
old errors after compatibility repair. Expensive assembled checks also reach
the resource boundary before migration.
The HE real-file check has byte-identical output on both versions: three
supported cases pass; equal-chain fails on both and is not a regression.

The verified source fingerprints and baseline classifications are recorded in
\src{lean/upgrades/4.33.1/verification.json}. From \src{lean/mettapedia/},
the recorded flagship gate is:
\begin{verbatim}
lake build $(cat ../upgrades/4.33.1/verified-modules.txt) \
  Mettapedia mettapedia metamathNIKAudit \
  checkRFC8259NativeForestExact sumoNativeSourceCheck \
  Algorithms.MeTTa.Simple.ParserConformance
\end{verbatim}
\src{lean/bootstrap_local_repos.sh} clones pinned dependencies and replays
migration patches, preserving existing checkouts.
\src{lean/upgrades/4.33.1/replay.py} provides \texttt{--verify} and \texttt{--apply}.
Main checkpoint \nolinkurl{15deaf0b6} is integrated and verified in the upgrade
worktree; all 36 executable checks pass. The merge commit and coordinated
main checkout handoff remain pending.
