Trust boundary of the HOTG interpretation
========================================

The target theory is HOTG using Megalodon's Egal profile. Both checkers import
megalodon/examples/egal/PfgEAug2022Preamble.mgs as a signature. Foundation.sha256
pins the exact contents used by the qualification scripts.

That signature contains 1,752 Axiom declarations, including upstream library
facts. They are available assumptions of the imported signature; this pipeline
does not replay their original proofs. An individual theorem may use only a
subset. Zero document axioms means that the generated development adds no
axioms to this context. It does not mean verification from only the primitive
HOTG axioms, and agreement between two checkers does not remove their shared
trust in the imported signature.

Section hypotheses in the reusable libraries are discharged into theorem
parameters. For example, NatAlgebra derives algebraic laws from operations and
their typing and recursion equations. Concrete instances must supply checked
proofs of those premises.

The generated source interpretations and target reconstructions are distinct
from validating Lean's original proof objects. General proof-object transport
and a replay of the imported signature down to a smaller foundation are
separate qualifications.

A signature update requires reviewing its changed declarations and updating
the pin deliberately before requalifying the targets.
